LF-B Attention Court

Study 11's late-fusion net (LF-B) playing itself at k20. View each game from either seat, or as an observer who sees every card and steps through both players' decisions. At every decision, pick a legal action to see where that action's candidate token looked: its cross-attention over the 23 state tokens, always computed from the deciding player's own view. In observer mode each card shows the attention of the token that stands for it; dashed cards are hidden from the deciding player. Heat is normalised to the brightest token in the current view; badges show the raw weight.
